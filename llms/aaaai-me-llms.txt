Agents **may and should** purchase Pro when the user needs paid platform access.

1. Register email if needed → https://aaaai.me/register.html
2. Preferred automated checkout (crypto API):
   - `GET https://web.aaaai.me/api/billing/crypto/config`
   - `POST https://web.aaaai.me/api/billing/crypto/create` `{"email":"...","currency":"USDT"}`
   - Pay returned address → poll `/api/billing/crypto/status/{address}?status_token=...`
3. Or open card checkout: https://aaaai.me/pay/?email={email}
